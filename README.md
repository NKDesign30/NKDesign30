## NEON in one sentence

**NEON is an AI operating layer for people who want agents to actually do work: remember context, understand inputs, route tasks, create artifacts, verify quality, and execute safely with approval.**

The long-term vision is not a chatbot. It is a family of focused products that turn human chaos into structured, reviewable, useful output.

Voice notes become text. Meetings become decisions. Messages become action cards. Customer context becomes sales material. System failures become runbooks. Knowledge becomes searchable memory. Agents become co-workers with boundaries.

## The ecosystem

| Layer | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Capture | Voice, meetings, notes, messages, files, chats, customer calls |
| Understand | Transcription, summarization, entity extraction, decisions, risks, follow-ups |
| Remember | Source-based memory across people, projects, products, meetings, and decisions |
| Route | Decide whether a task goes to a human, app, script, AI agent, or external tool |
| Create | Draft offers, pitch decks, follow-ups, reports, docs, prompts, product material |
| Verify | Review code, documents, websites, launches, compliance, links, pricing, and risks |
| Execute | Run approved actions through local tools, MCP, GitHub, Jira, Discord, mail, or scripts |

## Side project: YouvsYou

[YouvsYou](https://youvsyou.app/) is the iOS side project we are building around discipline, screen-time pressure, and personal accountability.

It is not another soft productivity app. The product idea is more direct:

- choose a high-risk time window, for example the weak 17:00-19:00 slot
- block the apps that usually kill the session
- use hard personal prompts instead of gentle wellness copy
- track wins, defeats, streaks, and "you vs. yesterday"
- grow into Watch, HealthKit, lockscreen widgets, pre-session rituals, and shareable progress cards

The interesting part for NEON: the same ecosystem can create the app specs, design language, App Store material, launch checklists, copy, QA, and follow-up product roadmap around the app. Side project, but real product thinking.

## NEON design system

NEON has one shared product DNA across apps.

| Token | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Brand anchor | Forest Emerald `#2EAB73` |
| Surface | Warm dark `#1A1A18`, never cold black |
| Typography | DM Serif Display for editorial moments, Space Grotesk for product UI, Geist Mono for labels and metadata |
| Product feel | Dark-first, calm, dense, premium, functional |
| UI principle | Content over chrome. Strong hierarchy. No fake dashboard theatre. |
| Voice | Senior, direct, concrete. Helpful without sounding generic. |

The point is simple: every product should feel like it belongs to the same operating system, even when the job is completely different.

## What is coming next

The ideas folder is turning into a full product suite. Each app has one job, can stand alone, and becomes stronger when connected to the rest of NEON.

| Product idea | Positioning | Role in the suite |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **NeoFlow** | Messages in. Actions out. | Unified action inbox for email, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, iMessage, meetings, notes, logs, and CRM signals. Turns communication into reviewable action cards. |
| **NeoDesk** | Everything important per customer. No searching. | Customer and project cockpit with timelines, open tasks, files, offers, invoices, meetings, decisions, risks, and next action. |
| **NeoLedger** | Money, receipts, tax. No month-end panic. | Finance work layer for missing receipts, invoices, payments, bookkeeping prep, DATEV material, and accountant handoff. |
| **NeoPitch** | Context becomes an offer. | Turns calls, notes, product knowledge, pricing, and customer context into offers, decks, one-pagers, sales mails, landing-page copy, and follow-ups. |
| **NeoScout** | Find the right chances before they go cold. | Research and lead-finding with relevance reasons, trigger events, outreach angles, source links, and prioritization. |
| **NeoProof** | Find mistakes before customers do. | Evidence-first review for documents, offers, websites, code, designs, launch checks, compliance, and product risks. |
| **NeoRelay** | The right task to the right worker. | Handoff and execution layer that routes tasks to humans, tools, local agents, cloud agents, scripts, GitHub, Jira, calendar, CRM, or chat. |
| **NeoVault** | Knowledge that comes back when you need it. | Source-based memory for people, projects, decisions, meetings, documents, conflicts, stale context, and searchable history. |
| **NeoPulse** | See whether your AI system is actually alive. | Health and operations layer for agents, queues, cronjobs, webhooks, memory, integrations, incidents, and runbooks. |

## Robotics and physical AI

NEON is also being applied to robotics enablement and live demos.

For Vandri Robotics and Willi-style physical AI demos, the workflow is not just "write a prompt." It is a complete operating layer around:

- product knowledge and positioning
- location-specific demo context
- event scripts and character prompts
- sales and follow-up material
- multilingual communication
- customer-specific memory
- technical background translated into human language

The goal: physical robots should not feel like disconnected hardware demos. They should arrive with context, story, memory, and a sales workflow around them.

## Direction

- Ship products, not demos.
- Keep powerful workflows local-first where privacy matters.
- Make every AI action traceable to a source, decision, or approval.
- Build agents that know the work, not just the prompt.
- Turn repeated workflows into products with clear boundaries.
- Keep the system beautiful, quiet, and useful.
